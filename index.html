About Me

Hi, I'm Nayer Haggag, a rising junior in high school with a strong interest in engineering and design. I spend a lot of my free time in my school's design lab, building things like a solar-powered charger for a community in Egypt and tools that teachers actually use in their classrooms now. I like taking an idea, building a rough version of it, and fixing it until it actually works.

Projects

Website Development

AI Feedback Tool

Built during my internship at Symphony.is, an AI-native company. Students upload their work and Claude grades it against a rubric, then gives them a score, a breakdown, and specific things they did well and things to fix.

iOS App

ScanSave

A budgeting app for teens that reads a screenshot of your bank transaction history, so there's no bank login required. I built it after noticing a lot of my classmates weren't tracking their allowance or saving anything, and I couldn't find a free app that worked this way either.

Project Shams

Solar-Powered Charger

Built with a friend from my school's design lab, this solar-powered charger channels sunlight directly into a phone. We built these on our own time and brought them to Tunis Village, an underprivileged community in Egypt.

Independent Project

Solar-Powered Power Bank

An independent redesign of the original charger that fixes its biggest problem: needing direct sunlight to work. This version stores solar energy in a battery bank so it can charge a phone any time.

Class Project

Whiteboard Organization Tool

Designed with a classmate for our Design & Engineering class. This laser-cut, magnet-mounted organizer keeps whiteboard markers and a cloth in one place, and six units are now used daily in teachers' classrooms.

Personal Project

Lawn Mower Cover

A birthday gift for my grandfather, built entirely from materials and tools we already had in the house. It turns the unused space above his mower into simple, clutter-free storage.
