Solar-Powered Power Bank

Independent Project

The Problem

The original Project Shams charger worked, but it had two real problems. It only worked in direct sunlight, and charging straight from the panel was slower than just plugging into an outlet. If it got cloudy or the sun went down, the charger was basically useless. I wanted to design a version that could store energy instead and deliver it whenever you actually needed it.

The Approach

Working on my own, I designed a solar power bank that adds a storage buffer between the panel and the phone. A 9V solar panel feeds a buck converter that steps the voltage down to a stable 5V, which then charges a power bank module that handles safe charging and boosts the voltage back up for the phone. Six 18650 lithium-ion cells act as the actual storage, holding onto solar energy throughout the day so it's ready whenever you need it, sun or no sun.

The Result

The power bank was designed and put together as a working prototype. I haven't run any formal performance tests on it yet, but it's a direct response to a real problem I ran into with my own earlier project, which is really what good design comes down to: noticing what's wrong and fixing it.
