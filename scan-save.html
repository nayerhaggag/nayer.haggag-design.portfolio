ScanSave

iOS App

The Problem

I noticed that a lot of teens at my school who get an allowance from their parents don't really track their spending, and they rarely end up saving anything. I'd actually been looking for an app to help with this myself, but I couldn't find a free one that let me just import screenshots. Everything either wanted a linked bank account or expected me to type in every transaction by hand.

The Approach

So I built ScanSave to solve my own version of the problem, working alongside Claude to actually build it since coding on my own isn't really my strength. Instead of linking a bank account, it reads a screenshot of your bank app's transaction history and pulls out the merchant, amount, and category on its own. No bank login, ever. From there you get a home dashboard with your balance and weekly allowance usage, transactions that are already categorized, a savings goals screen with an animated progress ring, and an insights screen that breaks your spending down by category so you can actually see the patterns instead of just a total. Since it only ever touches a screenshot you choose to import, nothing is linked, synced, or shared.

The Result

ScanSave is fully built and working, down to the App Store copy and marketing screenshots. I couldn't actually publish it though, since Apple requires developers to be 18 or older to hold a developer account, and I'm not there yet. For now it exists as a finished, working product built to solve a problem I saw firsthand at my own school, and I'm planning to publish it once I'm old enough.
