Whiteboard Organization Tool

Class Project

The Problem

This project started in my Design & Engineering class, where my classmate Zein Hussein and I had to find and solve a real problem at our school. We talked to teachers and kept hearing the same complaint: whiteboard markers and erasers kept going missing or ending up scattered around the room, which made it harder to keep the classroom organized.

The Approach

Zein and I looked at existing solutions, then went through several rounds of prototyping and CAD modeling in Shapr3d before we landed on a compact magnetic organizer that mounts right onto a whiteboard. We laser-cut the final design out of plywood, hand-assembled it with super glue, and used neodymium magnets so it attaches securely to any whiteboard without needing any hardware.

The Result

The organizer is actually being used now. Six units are mounted in teachers' classrooms at school, holding markers and a microfiber cloth right where they're needed.
