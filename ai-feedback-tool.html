AI Feedback Tool

Symphony.is Internship

Visit Live Site →

The Problem

During my internship at Symphony.is, an AI-native IT company, in June and July of 2026, I wanted to explore what I — someone with no coding background — could actually build using AI. As an IB student myself, I knew a real problem firsthand: getting a grade back on an assignment doesn't tell you much. Students need to know specifically what they did well and what to fix against the actual rubric, not just a number.

The Approach

I'm not a technical person in terms of writing code, but the older, post-graduate interns at Symphony.is encouraged me to start using Claude and see what it could do — and what I could do with it. Working alongside Claude rather than coding it manually myself, I designed and built a web tool where a student uploads their work (a file or pasted text), selects the subject and assessment type, and grades it against the official IB rubric or a custom rubric they upload. Claude returns an overall score, a category-by-category breakdown, and specific "Strengths" and "How to Improve" sections written in language relevant to that exact assignment rather than generic advice.

The Result

The tool is live and fully functional as a working prototype, deployed at the link above. My goal now is to bring it to my own school so IB students can get fast, rubric-aligned feedback on drafts before they submit. More than the tool itself, this project taught me that not knowing how to code doesn't mean I can't build real software — by learning to direct AI effectively, I was able to design, iterate, and ship a working product end-to-end.
