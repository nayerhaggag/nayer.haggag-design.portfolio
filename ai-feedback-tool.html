AI Feedback Tool

Website Development

Visit Live Site →

The Problem

I did an internship at Symphony.is, an AI-native IT company, in June and July of 2026, and I wanted to see what I could actually build using AI even though I don't have a coding background. As a student, I knew the problem firsthand. Teachers can't be available 24/7, but a lot of the time you need feedback on your work late at night, right before a deadline. It's not really fair to email a teacher after hours and expect them to respond, so students are usually just left without a clear sense of what they did well and what still needs fixing.

The Approach

I'm not a technical person when it comes to writing code, but some of the older, post-graduate interns at Symphony.is encouraged me to start using Claude and just see what it could do. So instead of coding it manually myself, I worked alongside Claude to design and build a web tool. A student uploads their work, either a file or pasted text, picks the subject and assessment type, and it gets graded against an official rubric or a custom one they upload. Claude gives back an overall score, a breakdown by category, and specific "Strengths" and "How to Improve" sections that are actually about that assignment instead of generic advice.

The Result

The tool is live and fully working as a prototype, you can try it at the link above. My goal now is to bring it to my own school so students can get fast, rubric-aligned feedback on drafts before they submit them. What I actually took away from this project is that not knowing how to code doesn't mean I can't build real software. By learning how to direct AI well, I was able to design, iterate, and ship a working product from start to finish.
