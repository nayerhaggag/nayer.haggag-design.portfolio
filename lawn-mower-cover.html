Lawn Mower Cover

Personal Project

The Problem

For my grandfather's birthday, I wanted to build him something useful instead of just buying a gift. His garage didn't have any real space for his lawn mower, so it was always buried under clutter.

The Approach

I gave myself one rule: only use materials and tools that were already in the house. I used plywood and a roughly 3mm sheet of wood, cut the pieces with a hand saw and a circular saw, then put it all together with wood glue and nails. The final design turns the empty vertical space above the mower into a simple storage cover.

The Result

My grandfather loved it. We don't live in the same country anymore, he's in the US and I'm in Egypt, but he still sends me pictures of it in use sometimes, which is honestly one of the reasons this is one of my favorite projects.
